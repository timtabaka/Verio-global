Solutions

From verification request
to trusted outcome in minutes.

Universities initiate a verification request, students and sponsors provide secure consent, and Verio produces a standardised financial verification outcome, without relying on uploaded bank statements.

1
University initiates
Admissions enters the applicant's details, names any sponsor(s), and sends the invitation. Everything anchors to the application ID.
2 minutes
2
Student & sponsor consent
Each party gets a secure link, reviews the consent, and signs in directly with their bank. Verio never sees the password.
5 min per party
3
Data verified directly from the bank
Balance and holding period are read directly from the bank via India's AA network and assessed against institution-defined financial requirements and applicable destination-country policies.
Seconds
4
Certificate issued
A digitally signed PDF with a verification URL is delivered to admissions. Students can revisit their status anytime.
Independently verifiable
The output

One certificate. A trusted financial outcome.

Each completed verification produces a digitally signed financial verification certificate with clear outcomes, supporting evidence and an auditable verification record.

Independently verifiable

Built for real-world admissions

Designed for how international students actually finance their studies.

Funds come from savings, fixed deposits, parents, sponsors, sometimes all at once. Verio handles the full range so families and admissions teams don't have to work around the product.

Multi-party consent
Student, parent, sponsor, any combination. Each party declares intent and consents separately. All tied back to one application.
Eligible financial accounts
Supports a broad range of eligible financial accounts through participating financial institutions.
Stability analysis
Balances are checked for consistency across the holding period configured by the institution and any applicable destination-country policy.
India-resident processing
Raw financial data never leaves India. Only a derived verification certificate crosses borders, aligned with RBI's data residency requirements.
Full audit trail
Every consent, fetch, review, and export is logged. Ready for internal audit, regulator requests, or infosec review at any time.
Role-based team access
Invite admissions officers, grant approval permissions, and control who can view or export certificates. SSO supported.
