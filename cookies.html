1. About this Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Verio Global Pte. Ltd. ("Verio", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on www.verio.global and through Verio's online services.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy PolicyCookie Policy, which explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal data.

2. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags and other tools that store information on, or access information from, a device.

These technologies can support functions such as authenticating users, maintaining secure sessions, preventing fraud, remembering preferences and understanding how a website or service is used.

3. Technologies we currently use

Verio does not currently use analytics, advertising or marketing cookies or similar tracking technologies on its public website or platform.

Verio's online services may use technologies that are strictly necessary to provide features requested by users and to operate the services securely. These may support authentication, session management, security, fraud prevention or load balancing. They are not used for advertising or behavioural tracking.

Where a strictly necessary technology processes personal data, we handle that data in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection law.

4. Analytics and other non-essential technologies

Verio may introduce analytics or other non-essential technologies in the future to understand how our website and services are used and to improve their performance and user experience.

Before activating any non-essential technology, we will update this Cookie Policy and, where required by applicable law, provide appropriate information and controls and obtain consent. Non-essential technologies requiring consent will not be activated before consent is given.

5. Third-party services

Verio uses third-party service providers to host, operate and secure its website and online services. The use of a third-party provider does not necessarily mean that the provider places cookies or uses tracking technologies.

If we introduce third-party analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies, we will identify the relevant providers, purposes and technologies in this Cookie Policy or our cookie-preference controls, as appropriate.

6. Managing cookies and similar technologies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Browsers may also allow you to clear website data stored through similar technologies.

Blocking technologies that are strictly necessary for the operation or security of Verio's services may prevent parts of the platform from working correctly.

If Verio introduces non-essential technologies requiring consent, we will provide a mechanism that allows users to accept, reject and later change or withdraw their choices.

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes to our services, technologies, legal requirements or practices.

When we make material changes, we will update the last updated date above and provide any additional notice or consent mechanism required by applicable law.
