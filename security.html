Built for Trust

Trust is at the heart of everything we do.

Verio helps education providers verify an applicant’s financial capacity using consent-based, bank-verified financial data. Our platform is designed to reduce fraud, improve consistency and provide institutions with greater confidence in every verification outcome.

Security, privacy and responsible data handling are fundamental to how we build our platform and work with our partners.

Privacy by Design

Verio is built on privacy-first principles that put applicants in control of their information.

We are committed to:

  • Obtaining explicit consent before any financial information is accessed.
  • Collecting only the information necessary to complete a verification.
  • Using data solely for the authorised verification purpose.
  • Maintaining clear audit trails throughout the verification process.
  • Restricting access to authorised personnel through role-based permissions.

Security by Design

Protecting sensitive financial information is central to our platform.

Our security approach includes:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest.
  • Secure authentication and access controls.
  • Comprehensive activity logging and monitoring.
  • Secure cloud infrastructure following recognised industry best practices.
  • Ongoing security reviews as our platform continues to evolve.

Security is embedded into the design of Verio and will continue to develop as we expand internationally.

Applicant Control

Applicants remain in control of their financial information throughout the verification process.

Where supported by local financial infrastructure and regulatory frameworks:

  • Financial data is accessed only after explicit consent.
  • Information is retrieved directly from authorised financial institutions or regulated data-sharing networks.
  • Education providers receive only the verification outcome and information required to support their assessment, rather than unnecessary underlying financial data.

Compliance

Verio is designed to support applicable privacy, financial data and regulatory requirements in each market where we operate.

As we expand internationally, we will continue to strengthen our governance, security and compliance framework in partnership with regulated financial data providers and trusted technology partners.

Responsible Technology

Technology should improve trust, not replace it.

Where automation supports the verification process, it is designed to improve efficiency, consistency and accuracy while maintaining transparency and appropriate human oversight where required.

Continuous Improvement

Security and privacy are ongoing commitments.

We continually review our platform, policies and operational controls to strengthen protection, respond to emerging risks and meet the evolving expectations of education providers, applicants and regulatory partners.
