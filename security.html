Built for Trust

Verio checks an applicant’s financial capacity on a university’s instructions using bank information authorised by each account holder. Where the university permits documents, a university reviewer assesses them and the result is labelled “University reviewed, not bank verified”.

The Security and Data Protection page is the detailed reference for implemented controls, regional arrangements and assurance. This page summarises that reference.

Privacy by Design

Each student and sponsor gives their own consent before a bank connection begins. Verio records the exact wording, time and verified email address. Financial information is used only for the requested verification.

Bank connections supply balances and their changes, bank and masked account details, and an account-name match. Banking credentials never pass through Verio, and individual transactions from bank connections are not retained. Uploaded statements remain visible as uploaded to authorised university reviewers.

No participant is shown another participant’s figures or documents. The Privacy Policy explains roles, access, sharing and rights.

Security by Design

Implemented platform controls include:

  • TLS 1.2 or higher in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest, including backups.
  • Database-enforced university isolation and specific permissions for reviewing financial figures.
  • Email verification and TOTP for staff, with a fresh second factor for sensitive actions.
  • An append-only audit trail, including staff reads of balances and documents.
  • Malware scanning and re-encoding of uploaded documents before opening.
  • Continuous monitoring, incident response, daily backups and automated security checks on changes.

The detailed security page sets out these controls, review arrangements and regional processing.

Student and Sponsor Control

Every participant authorises their own bank connection; anyone helping to pay makes their own declaration. A sponsor or guardian can withdraw their declaration and an applicant can end a verification. Either stops further reading and ends Verio’s access at the provider without changing information already supplied to the university.

Privacy requests and grievances go to privacy@verio.global and receive an answer within 30 days. Where Verio holds data for a university, the university decides the request as controller and Verio acts on its instructions.

For an applicant under 18, their own accounts and documents are not read until a parent or guardian has agreed using a verified email address. The university cannot disable this protection.

Compliance

Verio Global Pte. Ltd. operates under Singapore’s PDPA and processes financial and application data under each university’s instructions and data processing agreement. Verio is controller for participant sign-in details. India’s DPDP Act applies for Indian participants.

Financial data is processed in Ireland unless the source market requires processing in-market. The India-specific arrangement is proposed, subject to RBI approval and not operating today. It would process and store Indian financial information and backups in Mumbai, retain bank balances and account identifiers in India, and send the assessment outside India. University reviewers would see the assessment and could open uploaded documents, but not bank-connection balances. The proposed bank connection would use an RBI-licensed Account Aggregator through the licensed provider, with a separate consent artefact specifying purpose, data period and validity. See the India-specific arrangements.

University retention is 90 days by default, with 180-day or one-year options and dispute holds. Longer-lived outcomes, certificate records and audit trails have separate rules. The Privacy Policy sets out deletion stages, seven-day backup removal and contract-end export.

Responsible Technology

Verio applies the university’s rules and records the rule version. Cases the rules cannot settle, including documents-only cases, are held for a university reviewer. Admission decisions are made by a person at the university.

Continuous Improvement

The detailed security page is reviewed at least quarterly and whenever a control changes. Code-enforced controls are checked on every change.

Verio commissions an independent penetration test before universities are onboarded at scale and annually thereafter. Infrastructure providers publish their own certifications, which do not certify Verio. Verio will scope ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification where required by a customer or regulator.
