Privacy Policy

How Verio collects, uses, discloses, stores and protects personal information across our website and financial verification services.

1. Introduction

Verio Global Pte. Ltd. (“Verio”, “we”, “our” or “us”) handles personal information across our public website and financial verification platform.

For financial and application data, Verio acts as a processor on the instructions of the university requesting verification, under a data processing agreement. The university is responsible for the lawful basis for its assessment; each participant separately authorises the bank connection. Verio is the controller for participants’ sign-in details and handles website enquiries and business contacts for its own purposes.

Verio is a Singapore company operating under Singapore’s PDPA. Our university agreements carry the university’s applicable privacy obligations to Verio. India’s DPDP Act applies for Indian participants. Our Security and Data Protection page describes the platform’s controls and regional arrangements.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with Verio, we handle:

  • Names, contact details, verified email addresses, organisation and role, enquiries, and technical information such as IP address and browser information.
  • For bank-connected verification: balances and changes over the assessment period, the bank name, the last four account digits and whether the account name matched. Verio does not retain full account numbers, identity records or individual transactions delivered through a bank connection. Banking credentials are entered on the bank’s or provider’s screen and never pass through Verio.
  • Where the university permits uploads: supporting documents such as bank statements, sponsor, scholarship and loan letters, and fee receipts, together with the amount recorded by a university reviewer. Identity documents are not requested. Uploads are scanned for malware and re-encoded to page images before opening; the original file is deleted. Document contents are not extracted or stored separately. A statement image may contain transaction information as uploaded.
  • Consent and declaration wording, timestamps, verified email addresses, assessment outcomes, rule versions, reviewer notes and audit records.
  • For age safeguards: the under-18 determination, its source and time, and any required guardian agreement. The date of birth itself is not stored.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Deliver our financial verification services
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests
  • Communicate with customers, partners and prospective clients
  • Improve our website, products and services without using bank information for purposes outside the authorised verification
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our systems
  • Meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations

We do not sell personal information.

4. Financial Verification Services

Bank-connected verification obtains information from participating banks through the bank-connection provider for the participant’s market. The provider is named at connection, together with its regulator where it has one. Availability depends on the market, bank, account and supported data-sharing arrangements.

The proposed India route would use the Account Aggregator framework through a licensed provider acting as Financial Information User. After agreeing on Verio, the participant would approve a consent artefact at the RBI-licensed Account Aggregator specifying purpose, data period and consent validity. Consent could be revoked at the Aggregator. The Aggregator would carry encrypted information without being able to read it; Verio would connect to the provider, not directly to the bank or Aggregator. This proposed route is subject to RBI approval and is not operating today.

Every student and sponsor gives their own consent on Verio before a bank connection begins. The exact wording, timestamp and verified email address are recorded. Anyone helping to pay makes their own declaration about their own money; a student cannot authorise access to a sponsor’s information.

Where permitted by the university, documents may be uploaded instead of, or alongside, a bank connection. They go directly to Verio for a university reviewer to assess. These results are identified as “University reviewed, not bank verified”.

University staff see whether requirements were met. Staff with review permission may also see balances and how long funds were held, and open uploaded documents. The proposed India route would restrict university reviewers to the assessment rather than bank-connection balances; uploaded documents could still be opened by authorised reviewers. These India-specific additions are subject to RBI approval and are not operating today. See our Indian financial information page. No participant is shown another participant’s figures or documents. Named Verio staff may access production data only with a second factor and logged access.

Financial information is used only for the requested verification. It is shared with the requesting university and recipients the university names on a certificate it shares, with service-provider processing described below. Verio does not sell financial data.

Automated checks apply the university’s rules and record the rule version. Cases the rules cannot settle, including documents-only cases, are held for a university reviewer. The university’s admission decision is made by a person.

6. Data Security

Platform controls include TLS 1.2 or higher in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest including backups, database-enforced university isolation and review permissions, email verification and TOTP for staff, and an append-only audit log recording actions and staff reads of financial information.

Production access is restricted to named Verio personnel, protected by a second factor and logged. Monitoring, incident response, daily backups and security checks support these controls. See the Security and Data Protection page for the current details.

Infrastructure providers publish their own certifications; these do not constitute certification of Verio. Verio commissions independent penetration testing before onboarding universities at scale and annually thereafter, and will scope ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification where a customer or regulator requires it.

7. Data Retention

The university sets a retention period of 90 days by default, 180 days or one year, starting when the outcome is decided. A disputed record is held for the period of the dispute.

At expiry, balances, daily figures, uploaded documents, names, email addresses and phone numbers are removed. A participant’s sign-in address remains only to recognise them if they return and is removed at the six-year stage. The outcome, rules applied, bank name, masked account details for disputes, staff notes and audit trail remain.

Six years after the decision, those remaining records are removed except for the certificate record and audit trail. Audit records are retained for the life of the certificate record; they are not covered by the 90-day default.

Under the proposed India arrangement, retention, deletion and backups would remain within India, with backups clearing within the same seven-day period. Its Account Aggregator consent would expire on the date specified in the consent artefact, after which no further information would be read. These India-specific additions are not operating today.

Automated deletion runs daily and is logged. Deleted data leaves backups within seven days. When a university contract ends, its records are available for export for 30 days and are then deleted with written confirmation.

8. International Data Transfers

Financial data for UK and Australian destination workflows is processed in Ireland. The Indian financial information page describes a proposed arrangement, subject to RBI approval and not operating today. Under that proposal, Indian financial information, including uploaded documents, would be processed and stored in Mumbai using the Mumbai regions of Supabase and Vercel. Bank balances and account identifiers would remain in India. The assessment sent outside India would contain the calculated amount, holding period, rule outcomes and applicant name. University reviewers could open uploaded documents but would not see balances returned by an Indian bank connection. Other source markets use Ireland unless local regulation requires processing in-market.

Universities access financial information through the application; financial data is not copied to their systems through that workflow. Contract-end export is addressed in the retention section. Email and bot-protection services involve separate processing described below.

Transfers use the safeguards recognised by the applicable jurisdictions, set out in the university’s data processing agreement. Current regional details are maintained on the Security and Data Protection page.

9. Third-Party Providers

Platform providers include:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, storage and server-side functions, with data in Ireland or the required market region.
  • Vercel: application hosting and document scanning in Ireland or the required market region. Static files without personal data use its edge network.
  • Resend: transactional email processed in the United States. It receives email recipients and senders, university and bank names, message purpose, codes and links. It does not receive bank balances, connection figures or documents. Notes written by a person are sent as written.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: bot protection on sign-in and public pages, receiving IP addresses and browser tokens, not account data.
  • Bank-connection providers: the provider for each market is named to the participant at connection, with its regulator where it has one.

These infrastructure providers are United States companies. Verio has data processing agreements with its infrastructure subprocessors and agreements with bank-connection providers. Universities receive 30 days’ notice before a subprocessor is added. Provider processing and regional details are maintained on the Security and Data Protection page.

The public website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which receive technical request information when a browser loads their resources. See the Cookie Policy.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or objection, withdraw consent, or complain to the relevant supervisory authority. Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@verio.global. Requests and grievances receive an answer within 30 days. For data held for a university, Verio passes the request to that university as controller and acts on its instructions within the same period.

A sponsor or guardian can withdraw their declaration, and an applicant can end their verification. Either stops further reading and ends Verio’s access at the provider. It does not change information already provided to the university, and the record notes the withdrawal. You may also contact our Data Protection Officer for help. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing before withdrawal or applicable retention obligations.

11. Applicants Under 18 Years of Age

Age is established before a bank connection is offered. Where an applicant is under 18, none of their own accounts or documents is read until a parent or guardian has agreed using an email address they prove they control. The agreement is recorded verbatim. A university cannot switch this protection off.

A sponsor’s own funds can be assessed under that sponsor’s separate declaration. Verio records only the age determination, its source and time, not the date of birth itself.
